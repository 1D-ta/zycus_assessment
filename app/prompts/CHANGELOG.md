# Prompt Changelog

All prompt versions are tracked here for auditability and reproducibility.

## triage_v1.txt
- **Created**: 2026-08-27
- **Description**: Initial triage classification prompt. Classifies tickets by product area, category, urgency tier, relevant KB doc, routed team, and drafts a response.

## tam_extract_v1.txt
- **Created**: 2026-08-27
- **Description**: Stage 1 TAM extraction prompt. Extracts risk signals and verbatim quotes from the 90-day ticket window.

## tam_synth_v1.txt
- **Created**: 2026-08-27
- **Description**: Stage 2 TAM synthesis prompt. Generates executive summary, open risks, and talking points from code-verified quotes only.
